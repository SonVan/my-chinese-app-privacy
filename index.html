Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Privacy is important. My Chinese App stores your learning data locally and gives you control over optional backups.

Data Collection

By default, the app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. Learning progress, settings, and vocabulary data are stored locally on your device.

Google Sign-In (Optional)

You may optionally sign in with your Google account to enable cloud backups and use your learning data across devices.

If you choose to sign in:

  • The app requests access to the Google Drive storage needed for backups.
  • Your learning data is stored in your private Google Drive appDataFolder.
  • The app cannot access your other Google Drive files through this restricted access.
  • The developer does not receive or store your Google account credentials or learning data on developer-controlled servers.

If you do not sign in, the app works using local storage only.

Data Storage

Your data is stored:

  • Locally on your device.
  • In your private Google Drive appDataFolder if you sign in and use cloud backups.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google services for optional sign-in and cloud backup. Google may process information as necessary to provide these services, subject to Google's own privacy policies.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell or share your learning data with third parties. When you use Google Sign-In and cloud backup, Google processes information as necessary to provide those services.
